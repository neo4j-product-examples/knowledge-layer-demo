# Rosa's Bakery — Business Plan & Loan Purpose Summary

**Prepared for:** Meridian Bank, Commercial Lending
**Prepared by:** Rosa Alvarez, Founder & CEO
**Date:** July 15, 2026
**In support of:** Loan Application APP-3001 — $400,000 expansion facility

---

## 1. Business Overview

Rosa's Bakery (EIN 84-1023456) is a Food & Beverage business located at 214 Maple Street, Cedarville, OH 45314. Founded in March 2018 by Rosa Alvarez, the bakery has grown from a single storefront into a well-known neighborhood fixture, known for scratch-made bread, pastries, and a growing wholesale line supplying three local coffee shops and two grocers.

Rosa's Bakery has banked with Meridian Bank since July 2020, maintaining checking and savings accounts at the Cedarville Main branch. Over that relationship, the business has consistently grown deposit balances and has never missed a payment obligation with the bank.

## 2. Ownership & Management

Rosa's Bakery is privately held:

- **Rosa Alvarez** — Founder & CEO, 70% owner. Rosa has run day-to-day operations since founding and holds primary decision-making authority.
- **Marcus Alvarez** — Co-owner, 30%. Marcus is a passive investor and is not involved in daily operations.

Rosa Alvarez holds a degree in Business Administration and has 12 years of experience in food service management prior to founding the bakery.

## 3. Market & Growth Rationale

Cedarville's downtown corridor has seen steady foot-traffic growth over the past three years, driven by new residential development within walking distance of the current location. Rosa's Bakery has operated at effective capacity for its kitchen and seating for the past 18 months, turning away catering and wholesale orders it does not have the oven or staffing capacity to fulfill.

Customer demand data (point-of-sale trends, wholesale order inquiries) supports opening a second location roughly 2.5 miles away, in a growing mixed-use development, to capture demand the current site cannot serve.

## 4. Use of Loan Proceeds

The requested $400,000 facility (Application APP-3001, 10-year term, fixed rate) will fund:

| Use | Estimated Amount |
|---|---|
| Leasehold improvements / build-out | $220,000 |
| Commercial kitchen equipment (ovens, mixers, refrigeration) | $130,000 |
| Furniture, fixtures, point-of-sale systems | $30,000 |
| Working capital / opening inventory | $20,000 |

## 5. Repayment Ability

The second location is projected to reach breakeven within 9-12 months, supported by:

- An existing, growing wholesale customer base that can be served without duplicating fixed overhead.
- Six years of demonstrated cash flow stability with Meridian Bank, with no history of delinquency on any obligation.
- Conservative staffing and buildout costs benchmarked against comparable bakery buildouts in the region.

Rosa's Bakery is not seeking to refinance or consolidate any existing debt as part of this request — this is a growth-capital request against a healthy, established base business.
